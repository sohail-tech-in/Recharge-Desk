      The rules, built in.
      
      
      These come from the Calls Break Approval Workflow message. The planner checks them for you.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Posting window
      Post your break 30 minutes to 2 hours before it starts, because immediate approval isn't possible. Claude only posts inside this window. If your Claude app was closed and it's too late, it reports "Missed" so you can post by hand.
      
      
      First and last hour
      No breaks during the first or last hour of your shift. The workflow's example: a shift ending at 8AM gets no break from 6:45AM.
      
      
      Breaks and lunch
      The allowance is one 30-minute break and one 45-minute lunch. Split them, and don't take them together. Don't edit a break after it's approved.
      
      
      Who approves
      Your shift WFM. If the WFM isn't on the floor, ask the available SME or ATL. Breaks taken without approval count as unauthorized.

      What if I'm on leave or my plans change?
      Set a Slack status like "On leave" or "OOO" and Claude skips that day. To change times, update your plan here, switch the message to "Change my schedule", and paste it into Claude.
